Problem

A slow log-out workaround, client data kept apart only by the screen, and scans that failed without an error.

✎

Solution

An in-app account switcher, a server-side check on every request, and a completeness check right after scanning.

Results

Both Quantech fixes shipped before launch, and my switcher became Spin.AI’s fix.

Spin.AI

Switching client accounts meant logging out

Spin.AI protects Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 from data loss. Solutions engineers had to log out and back in to switch between client accounts. I confirmed with customer success that it was a real, repeated pain, then designed the in-app switcher that became the fix. Try both:

Quantech

Client data was only kept apart by the screen

Quantech builds tax software for accounting firms that handle sensitive client data. Before launch, I noticed firms were only kept apart by the screen, not by the system behind it. I proved it through the API, documented it, and engineering fixed it before launch. Try both routes:
